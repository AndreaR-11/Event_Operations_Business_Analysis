
# Event Operations & Business Performance Analysis
SQL and Excel findings | 50 synthetic event records | Financial figures presented in AED


## Business Question
What is the overall operational and financial
performance of the events business?


## Analysis 1 — Overall Business Performance

**Objective:** Understand the overall financial and operational performance of the business.

**Key Findings**

* 50 events generated 2,360 registrations and 1,932 attendees.
* Overall attendance rate was 81.86%.
* Total revenue was AED 142,750, with AED 68,500 in delivery costs.
* Net contribution was AED 74,250.
* Average participant feedback was 4.49/5.

**Main finding:** Overall performance was positive, with strong participant feedback and a net contribution of AED 74,250. This provides a baseline for comparing event formats, industries, and topics.

## Analysis 2 — Delivery Format Performance

**Objective:** Compare online and in-person event performance.

* Online events achieved 83.69% attendance, compared with 78.80% for in-person events.
* Online events generated AED 49,300 in net contribution, compared with AED 24,950 for in-person events.
* Average feedback was higher for online events (4.59 vs 4.36).
* Delay rates were 3.57% for online events and 22.73% for in-person events.

**Main finding:** Online events performed better across attendance, financial contribution, feedback, and delivery reliability. In-person events may need closer attention to scheduling and coordination.

## Analysis 3 — Client Industry Performance

**Objective:** Identify the strongest-performing corporate-client industries.

* Technology recorded the highest net contribution at AED 16,750.
* Financial Services followed closely at AED 16,600.
* Both achieved 4.69/5 average feedback and had no recorded delays.
* Healthcare had the lowest attendance (72.99%), lowest feedback (4.14/5), and highest delay rate (50%).

**Main finding:** Technology and Financial Services were the strongest segments. Healthcare requires further investigation, particularly around event delivery and participant engagement.

## Analysis 4 — Workshop Topic Performance

**Objective:** Identify which workshop topics perform best.

* Excel for Business generated the highest net contribution at AED 16,450.
* SQL Basics achieved the highest attendance rate (84.67%) and feedback rating (4.68/5).
* AI for Operations recorded no delays across ten events.
* Project Planning had the lowest attendance (79.95%), lowest feedback (4.43/5), and highest delay rate (20%).

**Main finding:** Excel for Business leads financially, while SQL Basics performs best in attendance and satisfaction. Project Planning needs improvement in delivery and participant engagement.

## Analysis 5 — Impact of Event Delays

**Objective:** Examine how event delays relate to attendance, satisfaction, and financial performance.

* Non-delayed events achieved 83.36% attendance and 4.56/5 feedback.
* Delayed events achieved 67.70% attendance and 4.02/5 feedback.
* Net contribution margins were 54.33% for non-delayed events and 33.95% for delayed events.
* Six delayed events accounted for five of the six delays recorded in Healthcare and one in Retail.

**Main finding:** Delayed events were associated with lower attendance, participant satisfaction, and contribution margins. The business should investigate scheduling, preparation, and resource coordination to reduce delays.

## Overall Business Recommendations

Based on the five analyses, the business should consider:

1. **Expand online delivery opportunities:** Online events showed stronger overall performance.
2. **Prioritise promising client segments:** Technology and Financial Services delivered the strongest results.
3. **Review workshop performance:** Maintain Excel for Business while exploring opportunities to expand SQL Basics.
4. **Improve operational planning:** Investigate delays, particularly in Healthcare and in-person events.
5. **Monitor performance consistently:** Track attendance, feedback, contribution, and delays across future events.
